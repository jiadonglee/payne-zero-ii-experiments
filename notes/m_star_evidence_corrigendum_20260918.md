## What can currently be claimed

M-giant dual-path initialization is a result worth keeping for a bounded
application test.  The working route is:

```
labels -> predicted (m,T) -> physical reconstruction -> unchanged solver -> spectrum
```

The network supplies a warm start.  Dependent fields are rematerialized.
The solver decides whether the atmosphere is acceptable.

On the 18 observed label rows (Teff 3050–3925 K, log g 0.5–2.5,
[M/H] −1 to +0.5, α = C = 0, ξ = 2 km s⁻¹; these are discrete rows,
not a claim that the intervening label box was validated):

- **18 unique targets**, not 21.  Gravity-walk closed three previously
  undetermined members of the six reproduction points; it did not add
  three new stars.
- **18/18** eventually reached spectrally consistent dual-path endpoints.
- **17/18** froze inside a 60-iteration emulator budget.
- **1/18** (`3750 K`, `log g = 0.75`, `[M/H] = -0.5`) froze at 90
  iterations.  Spectral agreement and budget compliance are separate.
- Frozen-precision cost is typically ~25 iterations (recorded range
  ~20–90).  Early 3–4 iteration formal stops are not the final cost.
- `t3250 g2.0 [M/H]=0` has a constructed reference from the independent
  gravity walk (emulator 40 / reference 30; TiO `<= 3e-4`).

This is internal consistency of learned initialization plus the unchanged
solver.  It is not a whole-grid success rate, external physical accuracy,
or a continuous inversion.

M-dwarf emulator v2 opened 9 validation points: 3/9 formally converged
inside 30 iterations, and **0/3** of those passed TiO.  It is not a
usable model.  It is also **not** a demonstrated 1D physical boundary.

## Engineering repairs in this change

- Cache reuse now requires `completed=true` and a matching configuration
  fingerprint.  `iterations.jsonl` alone is not enough.
- Temperature-response `protocol_hash` includes `mixing_length`.
- Parity-layer interpolation no longer silently fills with endpoints;
  report rows print the actual log m used and convert dex to fractional
  percent.
- Dual-path evaluate tables emit independent flags:
  `numerically_stable`, `flux_closed`, `structure_compared` /
  `structure_within_recorded_envelope`, `spectrum_consistent`,
  `within_budget`.  Legacy `verdict` remains a convenience collapse.
  The frozen flux gate (p95 ~9.56%, max ~23.15% on the dwarf-era
  certification) is not high-precision energy closure.
